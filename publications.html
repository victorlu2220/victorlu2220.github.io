Dopamine-Conjugated Methacrylated Gelatin Hydrogel—Physical, Mechanical, and Biological Properties

Lu, W., Rahimnejad, M., Ometto Sahadi, B., & Bottino, M. C. (2025). Gels, 11(7), 499.

GelMA–dopamine conjugates were developed and characterized as a tissue adhesive alternative to sutures, evaluating swelling, degradation, mechanical strength, and cytocompatibility. The optimal formulation crosslinked within five minutes and showed non-cytotoxic, shear-thinning behavior suitable for injection and bioprinting.

Scalable High-Density Tactile Arenas for Measuring Ground-Contact Forces in Freely Moving Animals

Lu, W., Slepyan, A., Perkins, P., Ascani Orsini, A., Quinn, K., & Thakor, N. V. (2026). Manuscript in preparation.

A modular floor of 16,384 pressure-sensing taxels measures ground-contact forces of freely moving animals at ~1 kHz alongside synchronized video, revealing load-sharing deficits after peripheral nerve injury that timing alone misses.
