Mussel Inspired Bioadhesive

✓ Published in Gels — read the paper →

Figure 1. Schematics Diagram for Polymer Synthesis.

Dopamine-conjugated GelMA was synthesized in five steps:

  1. Prepare 50mM MES buffer at pH 4.5 and degass it in an ultrasonic bath for an hour.
  2. Add GelMA, EDC, and NHS to the buffer with stirring and heating—EDC/NHS activate GelMA's carboxyl groups for the next step.
  3. Dissolve dopamine hydrochloride separately in MES buffer at pH 3.3, then add it to the mixture.
  4. Let the reaction run for a day, stirring in the dark (dopamine oxidizes in light).
  5. Purify by dialysis—first against DI water at pH 3, then at pH 5—and lyophilize to get the final dry product.

Characterization: rheology (shear thinning, temperature sweep, amplitude sweep) measured viscosity, thermal stability, and mechanical behavior under different conditions. Compression, shear, and peeling tests then checked how the material held up under normal and shear stresses.
